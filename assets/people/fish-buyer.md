# Fish buyer

The body, game-engine skeleton, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes and hair come from MakeHuman's CC0 system assets. The shirt is from `male_casualsuit03`, the jeans from `male_casualsuit04` and the boots from `shoes03`. The skin uses the CC0 `middleage_caucasian_male` texture from MakeHuman's system asset pack, with new face proportions and a dock-specific tint. [MakeHuman asset license](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md#c-the-license-for-the-bundled-assets)

The source GLBs were exported by CharacterCreator at commit `852e48f316b90a0937234886630e4fb4cdfd1f78`. The hat, apron, proportions and dock animations were built for Emerald Bayou.
